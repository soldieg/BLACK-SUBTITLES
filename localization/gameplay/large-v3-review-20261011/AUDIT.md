# Gameplay audio language and translation audit — 2026-10-11

## Scope and evidence

The isolated audio extraction in `.work/character-audio-catalog-20261011/` contains 4,163 XAS clips and 1,385 AWD streams. For this pass, the selected speech manifest contributed 3,547 voice clips. Twelve `E_DieHard2_*` / `E_Commando_*` AWD streams were retained as separate voice candidates because the asset names alone do not establish that they contain dialogue.

Two multilingual ASR passes were written to parallel files under `.work/character-audio-catalog-20261011/`:

- `transcripts-gameplay-multilingual.jsonl`: 3,559 records from faster-whisper base multilingual.
- `transcripts-gameplay-large-turbo.jsonl`: 3,559 records from faster-whisper large-v3-turbo. Russian and English hints were passed from the first detector; other records used automatic detection.

An independent manifest audit found 3,559 unique keys, one row per expected entry, no transcription errors, and no source WAV hash mismatches. The large-v3-turbo result contains text for all 3,559 records, including possible hallucinations on short or non-speech clips; this is not proof that every record is dialogue.
