# Russian cutscene subtitle drafts

The nine tracks here translate the repository's English SRTs into Russian: `03_N`, `05_N`, `07_N`, `09_N`, `11_N`, `13_N`, `15_N`, `17_N`, and `18_N`. English is the primary source. Existing Spanish, French, German, and Italian localizations were not used as substitute source text; they can be consulted later only where a line clearly corresponds and helps resolve context. Cue order and timestamps are carried over from the English source. Names, uncertainty markers, and speaker labels were translated consistently where possible.

These are AI-generated translation drafts, not official Russian transcripts. They were translated primarily from English because the supplied language archive does not provide verified Russian cutscene subtitles. No Russian cutscene audio was used to confirm wording, speaker attribution, or timing. The English source itself contains uncertain passages; those are retained as bracketed uncertainties in Russian. Native-speaker review and direct-audio verification remain pending.

`AK_N` and `MP5_N` remain without tracks because the existing collection identifies no dialogue for those clips. No audio or video is distributed here. SHA-256 values for these Russian drafts are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
