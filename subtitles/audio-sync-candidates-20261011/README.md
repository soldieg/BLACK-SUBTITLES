# Localized cutscene stream candidates

These 44 separate SRT files are exploratory transcript/timing candidates generated from language-specific audio streams. The stream-to-video pairing proposals are listed in `stream-sync-index.csv`. The cue wording is automatic speech recognition output and may contain errors in names, military terms, clipped lines, and language identification.

The available narrative streams were identified as English (`en`), Spanish (`es`), French (`fr`), German (`de`), and Italian (`it`). No Brazilian Portuguese dub stream was identified; the primary `subtitles/pt-BR/` tracks are translated captions over non-Portuguese audio. The `17_N` fifth-stream candidate was identified as Galician with low confidence, so no Spanish `17_N.srt` was emitted.

The files are intentionally separate from the primary translated tracks. No audio is included. Review the transcript and timing against your own lawful source files before using these candidates.
